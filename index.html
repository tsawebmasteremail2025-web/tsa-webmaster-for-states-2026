Our Mission

In a community as meaningful and beautiful as Gettysburg, everyone should be supported. The Gettysburg Community Resource Hub was created to help people find local services, trusted organizations, and opportunities to get involved without confusion or delay.

By connecting community members to support in one accessible place, this website aims to reflect not only Gettysburg’s beauty and history, but also its compassion, strength, and commitment to helping others.
